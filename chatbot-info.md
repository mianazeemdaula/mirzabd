## 3. Shipping & Delivery Policies

* **Delivery Locations:** We deliver to all cities, towns, and regions across Pakistan.
* **Standard Delivery Charge:** Flat PKR 200 for all orders.
* **Free Shipping Threshold:** Orders with a subtotal of PKR 2,000 or above qualify for **Free Shipping** automatically.
* **Estimated Delivery Time:** 
  * Lahore: 1–2 business days.
  * Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: 2–3 business days.
  * Other major cities and rural areas: 3–5 business days.
* **Shipping Partners:** Leopards Courier, TCS, and Trax.

## 4. Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

* **Returns Period:** We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy from the date of delivery.
* **Conditions for Return:**
  * Books must be in their original, unread condition (no spine creases, tears, markings, or folded pages).
  * Proof of purchase (order number or email invoice) must be provided.
* **Refund Process:** Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned books. Refunds are sent via Bank Transfer or EasyPaisa/JazzCash.
* **Cancellations:** You can cancel your order at any time before it is marked as "SHIPPED". To cancel, please visit your Account Dashboard or contact support immediately with your order number.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Do you sell used or second-hand books?
A: No, we specialize primarily in brand-new, high-quality paperback and hardcover editions. However, we do have a dedicated **Rare & Vintage** section for collectors containing out-of-print titles.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept:
1. **Cash on Delivery (COD)** (available all over Pakistan).
2. **Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex)** processed securely via Stripe.

### Q: Can I place an order via WhatsApp?
A: Yes! You can message us on WhatsApp at **+92 300 1234567** with the book names, your name, shipping address, and phone number, and our team will create the order for you.

### Q: What should I do if my book arrives damaged?
A: We wrap all books in protective bubble mailers. However, if a book is damaged during transit, please email us photos of the damage at **support@mirzabd.com** within 24 hours of receipt, and we will send a replacement free of charge.

### Q: Can I request a book that is currently out of stock or not listed?
A: Absolutely! Please contact us via email or WhatsApp with the title and author. We have relationships with international publishers and can import custom titles within 3–4 weeks.
